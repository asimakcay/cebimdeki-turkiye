# Cebimdeki Türkiye — v10 "Cep Kitabı" design notes

## Research (Oct 2026)
- **Wallpaper\* City Guides** (Phaidon, art dir. Loran Stosskopf): tight 8-unit Swiss grid; captions sit in one unit; rhythm comes from a few fixed picture depths/widths, not decoration; restrained two-font hierarchy; tabbed, step-cut sections to thumb between; colour used for *orientation* (coded sections/maps), not ornament; uncoated paper feel. (designweek.co.uk, dandad.org)
- **Monocle Travel Guides**: dense directory listings balanced with essays and photography; numbered entries that key to maps; short sections each opening with an intro; cloth cover, single colour per edition. (monocle.com, arun.is)
- **Lonely Planet / DK Eyewitness**: numbered listings, "Eat / Drink" sidebars with symbols, top-sights callouts, running heads and page-number-like references.
- **Michelin Green Guide**: star system + compact symbol key; margin icons; two-column text with small-caps headings.
- **TURİNG (Türkiye Turing ve Otomobil Kurumu)**: published Türkiye's first tourism guide *Turizm Kılavuzu* (1934–35, small format, 428 pp.), first road maps and first tourism posters drawn by period painters/graphic artists (e.g. İhap Hulusi Görey): flat printed inks, few colours, big lettering, bold silhouette. (turkiyeturizmansiklopedisi.com, istanbultarihi.ist, LoC)

## Principles applied
1. **Paper & ink**: warm paper tone (#F3EDE2), ink-black text, only two printed inks — İznik cobalt (#1E3A8A family) and brick (#A33A22). No gradients, no shadows.
2. **Type does the work**: Newsreader (text serif, optical sizes) for titles/body; Barlow Condensed for labels, numerals, running heads, distances.
3. **Running heads** in letter-spaced small caps (EGE · AYDIN · YENİPAZAR) above every entry/page.
4. **Numbered listings** (01, 02 …) like guidebook entries, separated by hairline rules, not cards.
5. **Distances as page numbers**: condensed numerals set flush right.
6. **Book plates**: photos printed full-bleed/flat, caption + credit line in small caps beneath (attribution kept).
7. **Chapter openers**: province screen = huge province name, plate code as an oversized numeral, region as running head.
8. **Text furniture**: drop cap on *Tarih*, pull-quote for *Efsane*, margin-note "Eat" sidebar (brick rule + ⁂-style symbol key) for *Yakında ye* / restaurants / mola.
9. **Tab strip**: bottom nav as a book's index tabs — hairline top rule, active tab marked with a cobalt bar and ink label.
10. **No-photo plate**: framed typographic plate with the initial in italic serif and a small caps category — not a gradient.

## Avoided (AI-template tells)
Rounded cards with drop shadows, pill chips everywhere, gradient hero overlays, centered generic hero, uniform spacing, emoji, Inter/Nunito-type UI fonts, boxing everything.
